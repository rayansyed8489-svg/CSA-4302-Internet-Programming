Railway Ticket Cancellation & Refund Rules

In accordance with railway passenger tariff regulations, refundable percentages depend upon the cancellation timing relative to the scheduled train departure:

Cancellation Timeline Refundable Percentage Clerkage / Cancellation Charge
More than 48 hours prior to train departure 80% Refundable 20% flat clerkage fee deducted
Between 12 to 48 hours prior to train departure 50% Refundable 50% cancellation fee deducted
Less than 12 hours prior to train departure 25% Refundable 75% cancellation fee deducted
Post-departure (Train has departed) 0% Non-Refundable No refund admissible
ℹ️ System Refund Execution: Upon submitting a cancellation request using your PNR Number, the refundable amount is automatically processed by the system and recorded in the passenger's account statement.
